# Deadly Assault copy and color contract

This is the bounded presentation contract for the current Deadly Assault page. It records only distinctions the current UI needs.

## Copy inventory

- **Official wording:** Deadly Assault, Trial Mode, Adversity Mode, boss names, element names, Specialty names, buff names, combat terms, and the exact mechanic and buff source wording. Keep these verbatim.
- **Sourced paraphrase:** matchup labels, `Suitable specialty`, fight-mechanic summaries, the `Who benefits / Trigger or requirement / Payoff` buff read, phase comparison language, and observed-clear statements. These may simplify source material but may not strengthen it into advice.
- **Editorial voice:** navigation, section headings, loading/error states, methodology explanations, and footer language. Keep these literal and player-facing; do not present editorial cassette, terminal, storefront, or neighborhood metaphors as official lore.

Historical character appearance is descriptive. The UI must say which observed cohort supplies the denominator and must not call appearance a recommendation, ranking, or tier list.

## Semantic palette

- **Ink and neutral paper:** content, encounter surfaces, mode framing, dividers, and ordinary emphasis.
- **Red:** sixthstreet brand and section eyebrows only.
- **Teal:** links, disclosure actions, and current-rate emphasis only.
- **Green:** verified/current data status only.
- **Element colors:** element chips and literal element names only. Ice, Fire, Electric, Ether, Physical, and Wind each keep one stable color.
- **Specialties:** neutral text. Do not assign separate Specialty colors on this page.
- **Trial versus Adversity:** official labels, grouping, border weight, and composition—not an unrelated card color.

No encounter identity may depend on list position, and prose must not become rainbow-highlighted. A retained color must have exactly one of the meanings above.
